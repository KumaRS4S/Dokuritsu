The Tours

Pick your route.

01

The Shakedown

from ¥40,000
Half day · ~4 hours · Shops across Kanto

Shop day — the visits and the buying. Tell me whose parts are on your car, or whose you wish were, and I'll build the route: tuning houses, showrooms, and the secondhand shops where the real finds are. Some stops are pilgrimage, some are shopping, and I'll tell you straight which is which before we go. Chasing something specific — wheels, center caps, a part that never left Japan? Give me the list before the day and I'll find out where it all lives, so we're driving to it instead of hoping.

02

Night Shift

from ¥55,000
Overnight · 20:00–04:00 · Tokyo Bay & the expressways

The all-nighter. Evening rental pickup, a browse through A-PIT Autobacs in Shinonome, dinner, then out across the Aqua-Line to Umihotaru — a five-storey parking area sitting in the middle of Tokyo Bay. Back through the C1 loop, the Tokyo PAs, and down to Daikoku last. That order isn't random: Daikoku before midnight is tour vans and phone cameras. After midnight it's the actual meet. When we're done I'll run you back to your hotel, or you keep the car and the night goes wherever you point it.

A-PIT Shinonome Aqua-Line Umihotaru PA C1 loop Tokyo PAs Daikoku PA

Runs any night of the week. Weekends get crowded and occasionally closed, so I keep the schedule loose and the route flexible — if one PA is shut, I know which one everybody moved to.

03

Touge Day

from ¥65,000
Full day · Hakone

Out of Tokyo early, onto the Hakone Turnpike. You've seen this road in videos. It's better with your hands on the wheel. From the top we take the Hakone Skyline and Ashinoko Skyline along the caldera ridge — Fuji on your right if the weather cooperates, which I can't promise and won't pretend to. Coffee at Daikanyama with the other early cars, lunch in the mountains, and a second run through the loop if the group has the appetite. Pace is set to the least experienced driver, always.
